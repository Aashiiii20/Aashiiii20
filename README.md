I build AI-powered products and automation systems for enterprise and SaaS clients. Over the last 3+ years, my work has covered multistep AI agent workflows, RAG, MCP, and agentic app building with Claude Code and Lovable, on top of a solid automation base in Make.com, n8n, Zapier, GHL, and Monday.com.

I currently lead a team of automation engineers at **MSquare Automation Solutions**. I run client calls from discovery through delivery, train corporate teams on applied AI, and head the organization's Skool learning community. I hold an M.Sc. in Data Science & Statistics, and my research has been published by IEEE.

- **Agentic AI & RAG**: production-grade AI agents and RAG systems that automate data retrieval, summarization, and decision-making
- **MCP solutions**: Model Context Protocol integrations for context-aware AI interactions
- **Vibe coding**: shipping AI-assisted web apps and client prototypes quickly with Claude Code and Lovable
- **Workflow automation**: end-to-end integrations across CRMs, e-commerce, finance, and project management tools
- **Browser automation**: Axiom.ai workflows for platforms with limited or no API support
- **Education & community**: tutorials, live build sessions, and corporate AI training
